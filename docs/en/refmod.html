Using RefMods (saved references)

From H3cApp v0.7.7, a reference image, video or audio file can be encoded once and saved as a small file (.safetensors). This is called a RefMod. A saved RefMod can go into any generation as a reference and is never encoded again. The prompt needs no number such as <Picture 1> for it.

RefMods and ordinary references

An ordinary reference (a file in “Reference images, videos and audio”) goes through two steps in every generation.

  1. It is encoded with the video or audio VAE and handed to the part that draws the video (the DiT) as a reference.
  2. Images and videos are also shown to the text part (the text encoder), so that <Picture 1> or <Video 1> in the prompt can point at them.

A RefMod is the result of step 1 saved to a file. A generation skips step 1 and hands the saved contents straight to the DiT. Step 2 does not happen.

Ordinary referenceRefMod
Encoded at every generationYesNo (the saved contents are read)
Shown to the text partYes (numbered <Picture n> and so on)No (no number)
FileThe image, video or audio itself.safetensors (a few KB to a few hundred KB)
Can be made smallerNoYes, when made “Compressed”

As the text part is not shown a RefMod, it does not suit pointing at “this person” or “this object” by number in the prompt. It brings the reference's look and feel into the generation together with the words of the prompt.

What you need

  • The Ref2VA model. Making and using RefMods both need Ref2VA, as generations with references do. If it is missing, add it with the model's “…” → “Add Ref2VA…” in “Model Manager” (Getting started).
  • In the M5's fast mode, the reference int8 cache is used, as for ordinary references.

Making a RefMod

  1. Put the source file in the references. In the panel, choose “From images” and then “Reference images, videos and audio”. Add the image, video or audio to start from with “Choose Files…”.
  2. Press “Save as RefMod…” on its row.
  3. Set the name and the mode, and press “Create”. The fields are in the table below. Usually a name is all you need to set.

The RefMod is saved in the RefMod folder (~/Library/Application Support/H3cApp/RefMods/) and listed under “RefMod” in “Model Manager”. With “Replace the original reference with the RefMod” on (the default), the RefMod takes the original file's row in the references, in the same place. Off, the original row stays and the RefMod is added at the end; keeping both makes the same reference count twice. A video reference that also uses its sound keeps its row and gets the RefMod at the end, as a RefMod holds the picture only.

Making a RefMod is GPU work like a generation. Pressed during one, it starts when that generation finishes. “Cancel” in the sheet stops it.

The sheet

FieldDefaultMeaning
NameThe file's nameThe name in the list, and the file name
ModeFull (as encoded)“Full (as encoded)” or “Compressed (small grid)”. Audio is always Full
Resolution cap1024 pxAn image or video whose short side is larger is shrunk before encoding. Nothing is enlarged (a short side under 320 px is widened to 320 px)
Frames to keep16Video only. Frames picked evenly across the whole video, rounded down to 5, 22, 39, … (16 gives 5). A video of fewer than 5 frames uses its first frame alone
Grid long side16Compressed only. The longer side is pooled into this many cells. Fewer cells mean fewer tokens and less detail
Refinement steps500Compressed only. How many times the pooled grid is adjusted toward the full encoding. 0 keeps the plain average
Max seconds15Audio only. Uses up to this many seconds from the start

Full and Compressed

  • Full saves the encoded contents as they are. They match an ordinary reference encoded at the same size.
  • Compressed averages the encoded contents into a small grid, adjusts it toward the original and saves that. It has fewer reference tokens, so the generation computes less. Detail and motion are lost in exchange. It does not learn a person's face or body.

Examples made on an Apple M5 with 24 GB from a 768×512 image and 3 seconds of audio. The times are first runs, loading the VAE included.

MadeReference tokensFileTime
Image, Full38474 KB6 s
Image, Compressed (long side 16, 500 steps)408 KB4 s

The same image as an ordinary reference in a Landscape, Small generation was 117 tokens. An ordinary reference is shrunk to suit the output size, while a RefMod is used at the size it was made. A Full RefMod can therefore have more tokens than the ordinary reference. To keep it light, make it with a lower resolution cap, or Compressed.

Stacking images into one RefMod

Several pictures of the same character or object can go into one RefMod. This is the “stack” form of ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3Mod, and the published RefMods of people (20 to 22 photos at 512 px, Full) are made this way.

  1. Put two or more images in the references.
  2. Press “Stack Images into a RefMod…” under the list.
  3. Tick the images to stack and press “Create”.

The images are encoded one by one and placed in order, one latent frame each. In Full the first image sets the size, and the others are cropped at the centre to fit it. The resolution cap starts at 512 px, and reference tokens grow with each image. Up to 32 images go in one stack, and videos can't be stacked.

Four views cut from a design sheet (front, side, back and face) made a stack of 1,728 tokens and 325 KB in 20 seconds. The hair and clothes came out well, but the grid, labels and side-by-side figures of the crops came along too, and a prompt for a night-time outdoor stage still gave a sheet-like picture. Stack pictures that show only the character: no text or frames, and a background close to plain.

Using one in a generation

  1. Open the references. Choose “From images” and then “Reference images, videos and audio”.
  2. Press “Add RefMod…” and pick a RefMod from the menu. A file not in the list can be picked with “Choose Files…” in the menu.
  3. Combine it with ordinary references if you like. The order is the list's order.
  4. Write the prompt and generate.

A RefMod row shows its name, its contents (kind, size, tokens, Full or Compressed) and a strength slider. The reference tokens and the memory estimate under the list include the RefMods.

Strength

Strength goes from 0.05 to 1, and the default is 1. At 1, the saved contents are used as they are. Below 1, they are mixed with a blurred copy of themselves, so the lower the value, the more of the reference's detail goes and the weaker it is.

It is an amount of blur, not a weight such as “reproduce it at so many percent”. At 0.3 the reference's shapes and colours remain, but its detail is quite blurred. The arithmetic is that of ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3Mod's Apply node.

Bundles

Picking a bundle, a file of several RefMods, opens “Choose the bundle's members”. Tick the members to use and press “Add”: each becomes a row of its own in the references, with its own strength.

Audio RefMods

RefMods made from audio work too. Unlike an ordinary audio reference, they put no <Audio 1> into the prompt. An ordinary audio reference needs an image or video reference beside it; an audio RefMod can be used alone.

Writing the prompt

RefMods get no number such as <Picture n>. Numbers go to ordinary references only, counted from 1 for each kind. For example, in the order “image, RefMod, image”, the two images are <Picture 1> and <Picture 2>. Describe what the RefMod holds in words, such as “a red ball…”.

Managing them in Model Manager

Open “Model Manager” from the toolbar and choose “RefMod” for the list.

  • Each row shows the name, where the file is and its contents (member by member for a bundle).
  • The row's “…” menu has “Rename”, “Show in Finder” and “Move the file to the Trash and remove”. For a file outside the RefMod folder it is “Remove from the list”, which leaves the file alone.
  • “Open Folder” opens the RefMod folder in the Finder. A .safetensors file put there is listed the next time this opens.
  • A file picked with “Add…” is copied into the RefMod folder and listed.
  • Drag rows to reorder them. The “Add RefMod…” menu follows this order.

RefMods made in ComfyUI and elsewhere

The format is that of the RefMods of the ComfyUI node pack ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3Mod. Files made with its “Extract H3 RefMod”, and RefMods others publish, work as they are, without conversion. RefMods made in H3cApp load in ComfyUI too. Bring them in with “Add…” under “RefMod” in “Model Manager”, or put them in the RefMod folder.

  • Formats read: single files (formats 2 to 4) and bundles (format 5), with image, video or audio contents.
  • Unusable files: a file that is damaged or has the wrong shape (an image with two frames, for example) shows the reason in the list and can't be used in a generation.
  • Format made: H3cApp makes single files of format 4. It does not make bundles.

Published RefMods come with their own publisher's license and notes; check them before use. With a RefMod based on a real person, mind how their likeness and reputation are treated.

Projects, continuing and joining

  • Projects: generating with a project open copies the RefMod files used into the project, as with other references. The strength and the bundle member are recorded too, and “Restore Settings” brings them back. Deleting the original RefMod does not break the settings of the project's videos.
  • Continuing a video and joined generation: RefMods carry over like ordinary references.
  • Custom Clips: a clip with “Use the same reference images” off can take RefMods with “Add RefMod…” in its references. As in the panel, each row has its own strength, and a bundle asks which members to add.

Limits and cautions

  • RefMods count toward the reference limits (9 images, 3 videos, 3 audio, 12 in all) by what they hold. Reference audio, RefMods included, is at most 15 seconds in total.
  • A video RefMod holds only the frames picked when it was made (5 by default). It does not remember the motion of a long video as a whole.
  • Compression loses detail and motion. To keep a person's face clear, make it Full.
  • As the text part is not shown a RefMod, an image's background, text and layout tend to come along with the character. To put the character in another scene, make the RefMod from pictures of the character alone, or use ordinary references.
  • A RefMod stays as it was saved. After editing the original image, make it again.
  • To tell several people apart by number, use ordinary references, which get numbers.
  • These ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3Mod features are not in H3cApp: stacking the same RefMod several times, curves that change the strength step by step, the experimental path that turns a RefMod back into an image for the text part, stacking videos, merging or masking several sources into one RefMod, and making bundles.

The local API can list and make RefMods and generate with them as well. See REFMOD.md on GitHub for how.
